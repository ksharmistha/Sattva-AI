# ** Sattva AI: AI-Powered Mental Wellness Companion**  
*A  smart AI-powered mental health and wellness app designed for holistic emotional well-being.*  

## ** Key Features & How It Works**
### **1️⃣ AI Voice Chat for Mood Analysis**  
- Uses **voice tone, speech patterns, and sentiment analysis** to detect emotional state.  
- AI provides **real-time mood insights** and suggests relaxation techniques.  
- Tracks **mood trends over time** to detect patterns and potential mental health concerns.  
- **Emergency escalation:** If severe distress is detected, suggests talking to a professional.  

**Tech Used:**  
- **Natural Language Processing** (Hugging Face Transformers)
- **Sentiment Analysis** (DialoGPT, GPT models)
- **React Native** for cross-platform mobile development
- **Firebase** for real-time data synchronization  

---

### **2️⃣ Personalized Coping Exercises & Relaxation Techniques**   
- AI recommends **guided breathing exercises, mindfulness meditations, and journaling prompts** based on mood.  
- **Adaptive AI**: Learns which techniques work best for the user over time.  
- Can integrate with **Spotify/YouTube** to suggest personalized relaxation music.  
- Sends gentle reminders to **practice self-care** throughout the day.  

**Example:** If a user is anxious, AI might suggest **box breathing**, **soothing music**, and a **journaling prompt** to reflect on gratitude.  

---

### **3️⃣ AI Sentiment Analyzer & Mood Trends Dashboard**   
- Tracks **daily mood swings** and **long-term emotional patterns**.  
- Uses **NLP (Natural Language Processing)** to analyze journal entries, chats, and voice logs.  
- Provides **monthly mental health reports** with insights on **stress triggers and coping effectiveness**.  
- AI suggests **lifestyle adjustments** based on emotional patterns.  

**Example:** If a user reports **stress spikes every Monday**, AI suggests **adjusting morning routines for a calmer start**.  

---

### **4️⃣ Data Security & Privacy**   
- **End-to-end encryption** for **voice chats, journals, and wearable data**.  
- **No data sharing** without **explicit user consent**.  
- AI runs **locally on-device** (whenever possible) to **reduce data exposure**.  
- Option for **anonymous usage** with minimal tracking.  

---

### **5️⃣ Doctor & Therapist Redirection for Severe Cases**
- AI detects **high-risk emotional distress** and prompts users to seek professional help.  
- **Smart Referral System:**  
   - Suggests **licensed therapists** and **mental health helplines** based on location.  
   - Can **auto-schedule appointments** with integrated telehealth platforms (e.g., BetterHelp, Talkspace).  
   - Provides **mental health resources** and **emergency hotline contacts**.
